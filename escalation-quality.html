Learn more about scoring
Completeness checks the current required fields; a field that reads as repeated or placeholder text, or copies another field, earns no credit (the first copy keeps it) and is flagged. Short vague answers such as “see above”, “latest”, “n/a” or “ok” block readiness. Specificity gives 13 points for concrete detail in the problem, impact, timeline and OS fields, plus 7 for Severity (2), Service Impact (2) and Affected Systems / Users (3). Reproduction steps (or, for an issue that is not reproducible, a detailed timeline), gathered evidence and its location, and troubleshooting actions paired with observed outcomes earn the remaining points: outcome credit is proportional and is full when at least half of the actions have a recorded result. Results that copy the troubleshooting text block readiness. A collection plan alone earns no evidence credit. Recent changes accepts “None” when nothing changed. This rule-based check identifies information gaps; it does not verify technical accuracy or replace technician judgment.
