To raise the score

    Updates as you write and scores every dated entry of this case together. 85+ is Strong, 60–84 Needs detail. A field that reads as repeated or placeholder text, or copies another field, earns nothing and keeps the note below Strong, as do troubleshooting steps without recorded outcomes (full credit needs an outcome for at least half of the steps). Case details are checked for format: Service Tag (5–10 letters and digits), Service Request (6+ digits), OS version (contains a number) and Log Location (link or path). Triage counts service impact and recent change from the Triage and Handoff Summary dialogs or written in the Issue Description; owner and date count from Follow-up or the Action Plan. This rule-based check finds missing information; it does not verify technical accuracy or replace your judgment.

  How backups work
  
    Notes autosave in this browser. With a backup folder, Case Notes saves a copy a few seconds after each change while the page is open, keeps one dated snapshot per hour, and thins old snapshots by your cleanup setting. Manual backups and safety copies (made before every restore) are never removed automatically. Everything is in a ProSupportToolsBackup folder; screenshots are stored once in its images folder. Clearing browser data removes local notes, so keep a backup folder connected. See docs/BACKUP_GUIDE.md.
    

    

    


    Restore from backup
    

    Pick a backup to go back to. Nothing changes until you confirm, and your current data is saved as a safety copy first.
